Product Pricing Sheet (Sample / Fictional)

Tiers are billed per seat, per month, billed annually.

Starter tier: $12 per seat per month.
Includes up to 5 users, 10GB storage, community support, basic reporting.

Pro tier: $29 per seat per month.
Includes unlimited users, 200GB storage, priority email support,
advanced reporting, and single sign-on (SSO).

Enterprise tier: custom pricing, contact sales.
Includes everything in Pro plus a dedicated account manager, custom SLAs,
on-premise deployment option, and audit logging.

Add-ons
Extra storage: $5 per 100GB per month, available on Starter and Pro.
Priority phone support: $99 per month flat fee, available on Pro and
Enterprise only.

Discounts
Annual prepay: 15% discount versus monthly billing.
Non-profit organizations: 20% discount on Pro tier, verified via
application.
Educational institutions: Starter tier is free for verified students and
teachers.

Sales tax
Sales tax is calculated based on the billing address and is not included in
the prices listed above. A typical regional rate used for estimates in this
document is 8%.

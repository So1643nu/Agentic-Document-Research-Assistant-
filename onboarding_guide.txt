Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the team. This document covers what to expect in your first 30 days.

Week 1: Setup and orientation
Your manager will schedule a welcome call on your first day. IT will provision
your laptop, email account, and access to internal tools before you start.
You should complete the mandatory compliance training (data privacy and
security awareness) within your first three business days.

Week 2: Meeting your team
You'll be paired with an onboarding buddy who has been at the company for at
least six months. Your buddy is there to answer day-to-day questions that
feel too small for your manager. Schedule 30-minute intro calls with each
member of your immediate team.

Week 3-4: First project
By the end of week two, your manager should assign you a small, well-scoped
first project. The goal isn't speed — it's understanding how the codebase,
review process, and deployment pipeline work. Most new hires ship their
first small change to production by the end of week four.

Benefits enrollment
New employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health
benefits. After 30 days, enrollment is only possible during the annual open
enrollment period or after a qualifying life event.

Probation period
The standard probation period is 90 days. Formal check-ins with your manager
happen at the 30, 60, and 90 day marks.
